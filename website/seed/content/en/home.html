Features

Your phone, right where you work

HandLive brings what happens on your Android phone to the screen in front of you. Everything stays private.

Clipboard

Clipboard, both ways

Copy on your phone, paste on your Mac. Copy on your iPad, paste on your phone. Text arrives right away on the same Wi-Fi.

SMS

Texts at your desk

Read and answer your Android phone's SMS from your Mac, iPhone, or iPad, with the whole conversation in view.

Calls

Know who's calling

Incoming calls show up on your Mac with the caller's name. Decline or end them from your desk. Your iPhone and iPad show who's calling too.

Privacy

Private by design

Everything is end-to-end encrypted, and there's no way to turn that off. Only your own devices can read your data.

Setup

No account to make

Your devices find each other on your Wi-Fi and pair with a QR code. Away from home, an optional relay forwards data it can't read.

Open source

Open and native

Built with each platform's own tools and design, so it feels at home on every device. The code is open source under Apache-2.0.

Setup

Set up in three steps

No account, no cables.

Install

Install HandLive on your Android phone and on your Mac, iPhone, or iPad.

Pair

Your Mac, iPhone, or iPad shows a QR code; scan it with your phone. The keys never leave your devices.

Carry on

Copy, text, and take calls as usual. HandLive stays quietly in the background.

On every screen you use
