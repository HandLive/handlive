Today we're publishing v0.1.0-beta.1, the first public beta of HandLive. This release is for early testers who want their Android phone's clipboard, SMS, and calls on a Mac, iPhone, or iPad — and who don't mind telling us what breaks.

What's in the beta

  • A two-way clipboard between your Android phone and your Mac, iPhone, or iPad.
  • SMS: read whole conversations and send texts from your computer or tablet.
  • Calls: see who's calling, and answer, decline, or end a call from the Mac.
  • QR pairing, and end-to-end encryption that is always on.

What's not in it yet

Call audio on the Mac, the camera and microphone for the Mac, and Continue Browsing are still being built. A hosted relay for when your devices aren't on the same network comes later too. The beta isn't on the App Store or Google Play yet.

How to try it

Download the builds from the release page on GitHub. If something doesn't work, please open an issue on GitHub and include your phone model and Android version.
