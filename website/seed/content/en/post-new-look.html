HandLive now has a logo, an app icon, and a voice of its own. Here is what they stand for.

A signal fire on a mountaintop

The moment something happens on your phone, it shows up on the screen in front of you. The logo draws that moment: a flame on the summit, with its signal going out to the devices around it.

The colors of dawn

Flame red, orange, and amber over a plum mountain and a cream sky: warm and calm. HandLive should feel like a lamp left on, not an alarm. Buttons stay green, so the brand red is never mistaken for delete or cancel.

Never miss a signal

Our tagline says what HandLive is for: the code on your phone, the text from a friend, the call you would otherwise miss at your desk. The new icon comes to the apps with the next beta, and the brand files and guidelines are open source too, in the HandLive repositories on GitHub.
